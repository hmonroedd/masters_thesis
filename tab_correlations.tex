The $z$ dependent quantities are given at the sparger ($z=0$) and at the free surface ($z=H$); their variation along the column is what the pressure change ratio \Gp{} measures. Auxiliary definitions: $\Delta\rho = \rho_l - \rho_g$, $A$ is the tank base area, $d_t$ its diameter and $N_\mathrm{noz}$ the number of sparger nozzles.}
\label{tab:correlations_hydro}
\footnotesize
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.6}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.4cm} >{$\displaystyle}X<{$} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.0cm} >{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Physical quantity} & \textbf{\normalsize Relation} & \textbf{LIBRA Pi} & \textbf{Source and validity} \\
\midrule

\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{\textbf{Bubble formation and rise}}} \\
\midrule

Volumetric gas flow $\dot V_{g0}$ [m$^3$/s] &
\dot V_{g0} = \dfrac{\dot n_g\, R\, T}{P_l(0)} &
$1.8\times10^{-5}$ &
ideal gas law at the sparger \\

Initial bubble diameter $d_{b0}$ [cm] &
d_{b0} = 0.54\left(\dfrac{\dot V_{g0,[\mathrm{cm^3/s}]}}{N_\mathrm{noz}}\sqrt{\dfrac{d_{\mathrm{noz},[\mathrm{cm}]}}{2}}\right)^{0.289} &
$6.0\times10^{-3}$ m &
\textcite{kanai_establishment_2017}, \ce{N2} in \ce{NaNO3} at 643 K, validated for 3--10 $\mathrm{cm^3/s}$ per nozzle, reported in \cite{evans_physics_2026} \\

E\"otv\"os number $\mathrm{Eo}$ [--] &
\mathrm{Eo} = \dfrac{g\,\Delta\rho\, d_{b0}^{2}}{\sigma_l} &
$3.7$ &
definition \\

Morton number $\mathrm{Mo}$ [--] &
\mathrm{Mo} = \dfrac{g\,\Delta\rho\, \mu_l^{4}}{\rho_l^{2}\, \sigma_l^{3}} &
$1.0\times10^{-8}$ &
definition \\

Terminal bubble velocity $v_{g0}$ [m/s] &
\begin{aligned}
H_C &= \tfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{Eo}\,\mathrm{Mo}^{-0.149}\!\left(\dfrac{\mu_{l,[\mathrm{Pa\cdot s}]}}{9\!\times\!10^{-4}}\right)^{-0.14} \\
J &= \begin{cases} 3.42\, H_C^{0.441} & H_C > 59.3 \\ 0.94\, H_C^{0.757} & 2 < H_C \le 59.3 \end{cases} \\
v_{g0} &= \dfrac{\mu_l}{\rho_l\, d_{b0}}\,\mathrm{Mo}^{-0.149}(J - 0.857)
\end{aligned} &
$0.26$ &
Clift 1978, validated by \textcite{chavez_experimental_2021} for a single He bubble in \ce{FLiNaK}; requires $H_C>2$ \\

\midrule
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{\textbf{Profiles along the column}}} \\
\midrule

Hydrostatic pressure $P_l(z)$ [Pa] &
P_l(z) = P_\mathrm{top} + \rho_l\, g\, (H-z) &
$1.40\rightarrow1.22$ \newline $\times10^{5}$ &
hydrostatics \\

Bubble pressure $P_g(z)$ [Pa] &
P_g(z) = P_l(z) + \dfrac{4\,\sigma_l}{d_b(z)} &
$1.40\rightarrow1.22$ \newline $\times10^{5}$ &
Young--Laplace \\

Bubble diameter $d_b(z)$ [m] &
d_b(z) = d_{b0}\left(\dfrac{P_l(0)}{P_l(z)}\right)^{1/3} &
$6.0\rightarrow6.3$ \newline $\times10^{-3}$ &
isothermal expansion (\cref{as:uniform_temp}) \\

Superficial gas velocity $u_g(z)$ [m/s] &
u_g(z) = \dfrac{\dot V_{g0}}{A}\,\dfrac{P_g(0)}{P_g(z)} &
$1.40\rightarrow1.61$ \newline $\times10^{-4}$ &
ideal gas law \\

Gas fraction $\varepsilon_g(z)$ [--] &
\varepsilon_g(z) = \dfrac{u_g(z)}{v_{g0}} &
$5.4\rightarrow6.2$ \newline $\times10^{-4}$ &
continuity, $u_g=\varepsilon_g v_{g0}$ \\

Specific interfacial area $a(z)$ [1/m] &
a(z) = \dfrac{6\, \varepsilon_g(z)}{d_b(z)} &
$0.54\rightarrow0.59$ &
spherical bubbles \\

$1.3\times10^{-2}$ &
