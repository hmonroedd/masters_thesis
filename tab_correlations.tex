Bubble diameter $d_b$ [cm] &

Bubble terminal velocity $u_{g0}$ [m/s] &
\begin{aligned}
H &= \tfrac{4}{3}\,\mathrm{Eo}\,\mathrm{Mo}^{-0.149}\!\left(\dfrac{\mu_{l,[\mathrm{Pa\cdot s}]}}{9\!\times\!10^{-4}}\right)^{-0.14} \\
J &= \begin{cases} 3.42\, H^{0.441} & H > 59.3 \\ 0.94\, H^{0.757} & 2 < H \le 59.3 \end{cases} \\
u_{g0} &= \dfrac{\mu_l}{\rho_l\, d_b}\,\mathrm{Mo}^{-0.149}(J - 0.857)
\end{aligned} &
Clift 1978 \\

Gas void fraction $\varepsilon_g$ [--] &
\varepsilon_g = \dfrac{R\, T}{P_0 + 4\sigma_l/d_b}\,\dfrac{\dot n_g}{A \, u_{g0}} &
ideal gas law (\ref{app:eps_g}) \\

Mass transfer coefficient $h_l$ [m/s] &
h_l = \dfrac{\mathrm{Sh}\, D_l}{d_b},\quad \mathrm{Sh} = 0.089\,\mathrm{Re}^{0.69}\,\mathrm{Sc}^{0.33} &
Briggs 1970 \\

Liquid axial dispersion $E_l$ [m$^2$/s] &
E_l = 0.678\, D_{t,[\mathrm{m}]}^{1.4}\, u_{g0,[\mathrm{m/s}]}^{0.3} &
Deckwer 1974 \\

Gas axial dispersion $E_g$ [m$^2$/s] &
E_g = 0.2\, D_{t,[\mathrm{m}]}^{2}\, u_{g0,[\mathrm{m/s}]} &
Malara 1995 \\
